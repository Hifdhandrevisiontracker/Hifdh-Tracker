My Companion — Account & Data Deletion

My Companion works completely offline without an account. If you created an optional cloud sync account, you can permanently delete your account and all associated cloud data at any time.

Option 1: Delete Immediately Inside the App

  1. Open My Companion and go to Settings.
  2. In the Cloud Sync Active card at the top, tap Delete cloud account and confirm.
  3. Your Firebase Authentication account, synced progress document, and synced text notes are permanently erased immediately.

Option 2: Request Deletion Without Reinstalling

If you uninstalled the app and cannot sign in, contact the developer via our official support channel at t.me/MyCompanionApp with the email address registered to your account, and your account and cloud records will be deleted within 7 days.

What Data Is Deleted vs. Kept

  • Deleted from cloud: Authentication user record, synced study plan, daily task history, and synced text notes.
  • Local device data: Audio recordings, media vault files, and inline photos are stored only on your phone and are never uploaded to our servers. You can clear them anytime via Settings → Reset everything or by clearing browser/app storage.
